## About

The [Penn Lifespan Informatics & Neuroimaging Center](https://www.pennlinc.io/) (PennLINC) builds reproducible software, open data pipelines, and scalable informatics tools to map human brain development. Its research integrates multi-modal neuroimaging, network science, and machine learning to define normal patterns of brain maturation and understand the developmental origins of neuropsychiatric illness. The center maintains major community resources, including the [Reproducible Brain Charts](https://reprobrainchart.github.io/) (RBC), the dMRI data of the [ABCD-BIDS Community Collection](https://abcdstudy.org/) (ABCC), and core analytical workflows like [QSIPrep](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34155395/), [QSIRecon](https://qsirecon.readthedocs.io/en/latest/), and [XCP-D](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40800264/).
