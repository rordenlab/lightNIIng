The [NeuroImaging PREProcessing toolS (NiPreps)](https://www.nipreps.org/) team develops a community-driven ecosystem of standardized, open-source pipelines for reproducible neuroimaging preprocessing. Its framework includes widely adopted workflows such as [fMRIPrep](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30532080/) and [MRIQC](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28945803/), alongside specialized pipelines for structural, diffusion, infant, and PET imaging. By decoupling preprocessing from downstream statistical analysis, NiPreps ensures transparent, robust, and reusable data processing across diverse acquisition parameters, clinical populations, and species.
