The [Douglas Neuroinformatics Platform](https://douglasneuroinformatics.ca/) (DNP) team develops open-source software and computing infrastructure for mental health research at the Douglas Research Centre. It operates a single-sign-on Linux computing platform for storage and computationally intensive analysis, which grew from the computing platform of the Cerebral Imaging Centre (CIC). The team also leads the development of [Open Data Capture](https://opendatacapture.org/), a web-based platform that collects clinical instrument data remotely and in person and supports measurement-based care at the Douglas, and [DataBank](https://github.com/DouglasNeuroInformatics/DataBank), a platform to manage, version, and share tabular datasets.
