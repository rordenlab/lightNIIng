The [Neurodesk](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38191935/) team develops open-source infrastructure for accessible and reproducible neuroimaging. Its eponymous [Neurodesk](https://neurodesk.org/) platform enables neuroimaging analyses to run across personal computers, high-performance computing, and cloud environments while addressing software dependencies, version conflicts, and platform incompatibilities.
