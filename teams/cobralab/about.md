The [Computational Brain Anatomy Laboratory](https://www.cobralab.ca/) (CoBrALab) at the Douglas Research Centre and McGill University studies neurodevelopment and aging in normal and disordered trajectories. The lab develops open-source methods for computational neuroanatomy in humans and rodents. These include the [MAGeTbrain](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22611030/) multi-atlas segmentation algorithm, the [RABIES](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39112455/) rodent fMRI preprocessing and quality-control platform, and optimized [template construction](https://github.com/CoBrALab/optimized_antsMultivariateTemplateConstruction) and deformation-based morphometry pipelines. The lab also shares high-resolution [atlases](https://www.cobralab.ca/atlases) of human neuroanatomy.
