The NiiVue team has developed and maintained free, open-source neuroimaging software for more than 25 years. Its visualization platforms include [MRIcro](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11568431/), [MRIcron](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39761848/), [MRIcroGL](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40707711/), [Surfice](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40707712/), and the community-driven [NiiVue](https://niivue.com/). The team also maintains core image-processing tools, including [dcm2niix](https://github.com/rordenlab/dcm2niix) and [niimath](https://github.com/rordenlab/niimath).
