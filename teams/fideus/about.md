The [fideus labs](https://fideus.io/) team has led the development of [ITK-Wasm](https://proceedings.scipy.org/articles/TCFJ5130), bringing the proven [ITK](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24600387/) scientific computing library to the web. Its browser-based platforms and tools enable high-performance medical image processing and interactive spatial-data analysis directly in web applications. The team maintains core infrastructure and spatial-data tooling across the open-source scientific software ecosystem, including community datasets in scalable, [web-optimized](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37428210/) formats.
