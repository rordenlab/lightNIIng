## About

The [DIPY](https://dipy.org/) (Diffusion Imaging in Python) team develops comprehensive open-source tools for analyzing computational neuroimaging data, with a strong focus on [diffusion MRI](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24600385/). Its library provides advanced algorithms for fiber tractography, microstructure imaging, registration, reconstruction, and denoising, making state-of-the-art processing pipelines accessible and reproducible across diverse computing environments.
